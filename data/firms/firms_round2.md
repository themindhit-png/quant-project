## NOTES AND CAVEATS

1. **HyroTrader "Restricted Jurisdictions List"**: Referenced in Terms (last updated 2026-08-07) but the page at /restricted-jurisdictions/ returned 404 on 2026-09-09. The FAQ articles clearly state HyroTrader itself imposes no country restrictions — restrictions on the Bybit platform come from Bybit's own ToS.

2. **Mubite minimum trading days discrepancy**: The challenge rules page (https://mubite.com/en/challengeRules) states **13 days**. The question noted the homepage sometimes shows 10 days. On the date of research, the rules page states 13 days — that is the authoritative current value.

3. **Fundedbit EAs**: Completely prohibited. A bot trader cannot use Fundedbit for automated trading.

4. **PTB Bybit page vs main page**: The Bybit landing page (https://plutustradebase.com/bybit/) is a marketing page for Bybit-platform traders. PTB supports six trading platforms. The statement "No simulator in between" refers to the funded account execution, not the challenge. Challenges run on demo accounts.

5. **PTB legal entity**: No legal entity name or company registration number is published on the pages crawled. The site shows "HQ in Limassol · Cyprus" and shows team photographs and video testimonials, but a formal registered entity name was not found.

6. **Funding fees (perpetual swap/futures)**: None of the four firms explicitly addresses whether funding fees (perpetual swap funding rates) are credited or charged on demo/simulated accounts. This topic is NOT PUBLISHED by any of the four firms on the pages reviewed.
