## Section 1: Breakout Prop

### Company Facts
- **Legal entity:** Breakout Trading Group, LLC (US registration 7403672)
- **Founded:** November 2023 by Alex Miningham and Dylan Loomer, Tampa, Florida
- **Key event:** Acquired by Kraken (major regulated crypto exchange) on September 1, 2025 — the first and still only prop firm owned by a major regulated exchange
- **Funding raised:** $4.5M seed round (July 2024) from investors including RockawayX
- **Is funded stage simulated?** Yes — evaluation is simulated, but payout obligation sits on Kraken's balance sheet
- **URL:** breakoutprop.com

### Trustpilot
- **Rating:** ~4.8/5 (one tracker noted a recent dip toward 4.5; treat as snapshot)
- **Review count:** ~860 reviews
- **1-star themes:** Very few; some complaints about Elite Turbo's 3% drawdown being punishing on crypto volatility; non-refundable fees after first trade
- **Source:** TrailingStopLoss.com review (July 2026), Velotrade, JoinProp profile

### Reddit / Community
- No credible pattern of payout denial reports found. Active 20,000-member Discord. No blacklist mentions on r/propfirms.

### Independent Payout Proofs
- **Zero verified payout denials** claimed and independently cited across 20,000+ funded accounts — repeated by multiple independent review sites (TrailingStopLoss, PropFirmsCompared, marketsxplora.com)
- Payouts in USDC (ERC-20) via Riseworks; blockchain-verifiable

### CIS/Belarus/Russia
- No explicit country restriction list published in researched sources. Kraken has KYC compliance requirements. Given Kraken's regulatory obligations, Russian/Belarusian residents are very likely blocked or face severe KYC barriers. No direct CIS user reports found.

### Red Flags
1. The 90% profit split is a paid add-on (selected at checkout at higher fee), not an earned tier — marketing claim versus reality
2. Fees are non-refundable once you place a trade — zero tolerance for second thoughts
3. No fiat payout rail; USDC ERC-20 only (gas fees on trader)

### Payout Reliability Score: **5/5**
**Justification:** Kraken ownership provides institutional balance-sheet backing that no other crypto prop firm can match. Zero verified payout denials across 20,000+ accounts is the strongest independent signal in the category. Clean rulebook (no consistency rule, no time limit, no minimum days) removes the most common excuse used to deny payouts elsewhere.
